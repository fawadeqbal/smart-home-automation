## "Microphone blocked" or "Microphone not available"

The browser or phone hasn't given Switchboard the microphone.

- **Chrome (Android or computer):** tap the icon to the left of the address, open **Site settings** (or **Permissions**), set **Microphone** to **Allow**, then reload the page.
- **Safari (iPhone):** **Settings** → **Safari** → **Microphone**, or the **aA** menu → **Website Settings** → **Microphone** → **Allow**.
- **Phone app:** the app says "Allow the microphone for Switchboard in your phone's settings — or type instead." Open your phone's **Settings** → **Apps** → **Switchboard** → **Permissions** → **Microphone**.

## "Voice needs https:// or http://localhost"

Browsers only allow the microphone on secure addresses. Use **https://home.fawadiqbal.dev**. Typing works on any address.

## "Sorry, I did not catch that. Please try again."

The recording was empty or too quiet.
- Speak after you tap, and tap again (or **Stop and send**) when you're done. In the web app, recording stops by itself after 15 seconds.
- On a computer, check that the browser is using the right microphone and that its input level isn't at zero.
- Move closer, and away from a running fan or TV.
- In the phone app, "I didn't catch that — hold on a little longer" means you let go too soon.

## It heard the wrong words or the wrong device

- Use the names shown on your tiles ("bedroom light", "kitchen fan"). The assistant is told your device and room names, so they're usually heard correctly.
- If you call a device something else at home, such as "tube light" or "batti", your installer can add that as an **other name for voice**.
- If two devices match, it asks "Which one do you mean…?". Just answer.
- Typing always works if voice struggles in a noisy room.

## Wrong language

The assistant understands **English and Urdu**, mixed if you like. If it mistakes Urdu for Hindi, it listens again as Urdu. It replies in the language you used, but short everyday commands are confirmed in English ("Done, the bulb is off."). Replies in Urdu script are read aloud in a simpler voice than English replies.

## "The voice assistant is not running"

This is a problem on the Switchboard server, not your phone. Switching from the tiles still works. Tell your installer (**Me** → **Message your expert**).
