## Put it right

1. **Switch it back.** Tap the tile, use the wall button, or say "turn the bedroom light back on".
2. **Remove an unwanted timer or schedule.** Ask "what timers are set?", then "cancel the bulb timer", "cancel number 2" or "cancel all timers". You can also open **Automations** in the web app or the **Routines** tab in the phone app and delete it there.
3. **Check what actually happened.** The device's **History** (and **Me** → **Activity** in the phone app) shows each change and whether it came from voice.

## Why it happens, and how to avoid it

- **Similar names.** "Light" can match several lights. Say the room too: "kitchen light". If two devices match equally, the assistant should ask which one you mean.
- **"It" and "that one"** refer to what was said just before in the same conversation. To start clean, press **New conversation** in the web app. A conversation also starts afresh by itself after about 30 minutes of quiet.
- **A misheard word.** Spoken requests can be transcribed a little wrong, for example "turn of" for "turn off". If voice keeps getting one device wrong, ask your installer to add the name you use as an **other name for voice**, or type the request.
- **Times.** "At 11" means the next 11 o'clock, so in the evening that's 11 pm. Say "am" or "pm", or "subah" or "raat", to be exact. Times use your home's time zone.

## What the assistant will never do

- Act beyond your rights. It can't switch a room you can only see, or touch another home.
- Claim something is done without the board confirming it. If it says "did not respond", the device wasn't switched.

## Report it

If the assistant keeps getting the same thing wrong, tell your installer what you said and what happened. Use **Me** → **Message your expert**, or **Diagnostics** → **Send to support**. Mention the time, so they can find it in the history.
