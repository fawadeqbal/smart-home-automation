## "Wrong email or password"

- Use the **same email** your invite was sent to.
- Check the password carefully: capital letters count, and so do extra spaces when you paste it.
- From the second wrong try, the message shows how many tries you have left ("3 tries left").

## "Too many failed attempts…"

After **5 wrong passwords** for the same email, sign-in is paused for **15 minutes** for your safety. While it's paused, even the right password shows "Too many failed attempts. Try again in 15 minutes."

Wait the full 15 minutes. Be careful with the next try: one more wrong password straight after the pause starts another 15-minute pause. A correct sign-in resets the count.

## Forgot your password?

There's no "reset by email" in Switchboard. What to do depends on your role:
- **Family members and guests:** ask the home owner. The owner can **Remove from home** in **People** and then invite you again with a new link, where you choose a new password. Removing deletes your account (unless you belong to another home) and the timers and scenes you made, and your room access must be set again.
- **Home owners:** the owner can't be removed and invited again, and the app has no way to reset the owner's password. Contact your installer.
- **Already have an account?** An invite link to a new home asks for your **existing** password. It doesn't replace it.

## "This account is disabled…"

The home owner or an administrator has disabled your account. Ask them to enable it again (**People** → **⋯** → **Enable account**).

## "Session expired. Sign in again."

You haven't used Switchboard on this device for 30 days, or you signed out. Sign in again.

## Phone app only

- Check the server shown under the sign-in button ("Server: … · Change"). It should be **home.fawadiqbal.dev**.
- "Can't reach your Switchboard server" means the phone is offline, or the address is wrong (see "The app can't reach the server").

## Never got an account?

You need an invite link first. See "Sign in for the first time".
