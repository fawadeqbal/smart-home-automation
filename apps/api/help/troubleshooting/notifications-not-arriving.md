## 1. Is the alert turned on for you?

Open the bell → **All notifications & settings** → **Tell me when…** (web), or **Me** → **NOTIFY ME WHEN** (phone app). Check that the alert is on. **Motion at night** is off unless you turn it on.

## 2. Should you get this alert at all?

- **Door, window and motion alerts** are only sent at night, **10 pm to 6 am** in your home's time zone.
- **Temperature alerts** are only sent above 40 °C or below 5 °C.
- **Board alerts** (offline, weak Wi-Fi, low voltage) go to owners and admins only.
- **Sensor alerts** go only to people who can see that room.
- **Repeats are held back.** After an alert, the same one isn't sent again for a while: 10 minutes for a leak, 15 for motion, an hour for temperature, 6 hours for board health.
- You need the sensor. No leak sensor fitted means no leak alerts.

## 3. Is it in the list, but not popping up on your phone?

Alerts always appear under the bell in the web app. Getting them **when the app is closed** needs one more step on each phone or computer:
1. Open the web app on that device → **All notifications & settings**.
2. Press **Turn on alerts for this device** and allow notifications.

If you see:
- **"Notifications are blocked for this site in the browser settings."** Open the site settings next to the address bar and allow notifications.
- **"Push needs https…"** Use https://home.fawadiqbal.dev.
- **"This browser cannot receive push notifications."** Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox. On iPhone, add the app to the Home Screen first and open it from there.
- **"Push is not set up on the server."** Tell your installer.

Also check that your phone isn't in Do Not Disturb or battery saver for the browser.

## 4. Using the Switchboard phone app?

The phone app saves your alert choices but doesn't show or deliver alerts itself. To get alerts on your phone, set up the web app as in step 3.

## Still nothing?

Notifications older than 30 days are removed automatically. If an alert you expected is missing completely, tell your installer (**Me** → **Message your expert**) with the time it should have happened.
