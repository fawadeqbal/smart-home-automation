## "This invite can't be opened."

The page says "This invite link is no longer valid. Ask for a new one." This happens when:
- **More than 7 days** have passed since the link was made.
- **It was already used.** Each link works once. If you joined already, just **Sign in**.
- **A newer link was made** for the same email. Only the newest one works.
- **The person who invited you cancelled it.**

**What to do:** ask the person who invited you for a new link. Owners and admins make one in **People** → **Invite someone** (web), or **Me** → **Family** → **Invite someone** (phone app).

## Errors while joining

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "Enter your name." | Type your name before continuing. |
| "Use at least 8 characters." / "Add a number or a symbol…" / "That password is too common…" | Choose a stronger password. |
| "Wrong password for this account." | You already have a Switchboard account with this email. Enter **that** password, not a new one. |
| "This account is disabled." | Ask the home owner or your installer. |
| "This home already has an owner." | An owner has already joined this home. Ask for a family invite instead. |
| "The owner must be the customer, not someone on the installer's team." | Owner invites can't be accepted by company staff. |

## Tips

- Open the link in a normal browser such as Chrome or Safari. If WhatsApp opens it inside its own viewer and it misbehaves, use "Open in browser".
- Copy the **whole** link. A link cut off in a message won't work.
- The email on the invite is the one you'll sign in with. If it's wrong, ask for a new invite to the right email.
- After joining, you can also sign in in the phone app. Enter **https://home.fawadiqbal.dev** when it asks for the server.

## For the person who invited

In **People** (web), pending invites show "Until …" with their expiry date and a **Cancel** button. To resend, simply create a new invite for the same email. It replaces the old link. Installers resend the owner invite from the home's workspace.
