A **board** is the small Wi-Fi controller your installer fitted behind a switchboard. When it loses power or Wi-Fi, the devices wired to it show **Offline**. Home shows a banner such as "**Living room is offline** — The wall switches still work. It reconnects by itself — your installer has been told."

## What still works

- **Wall buttons keep working.** The board runs from its saved settings without Wi-Fi.
- From the app, you can't switch that board's devices until it reconnects. Timers for them fail and you get a notification.
- Other boards in the home keep working normally.

## What to try, in order

1. **Is there power?** If the room's lights don't work from the wall either, check the breaker (MCB) for that room in your main panel. During load-shedding, the board comes back by itself with the power.
2. **Is your Wi-Fi working?** Check that other phones have internet on the home Wi-Fi.
3. **Restart your Wi-Fi router.** Boards rejoin by themselves, usually within a minute or two of the router being back.
4. **Did you change the router, Wi-Fi name or password?** The board still looks for the old network. See "I changed my Wi-Fi or got a new router".
5. **Wait a few minutes.** Boards keep retrying on their own.

## The board's light (if your installer showed you the board)

| Blue light | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Fast blinking for 3 seconds after power-on | Starting up (normal) |
| Fast blinking | Can't join the Wi-Fi |
| Slow blinking | Wi-Fi OK, but can't reach the Switchboard server |
| Off | Connected, or no power at all |

Don't open a switchboard to look. Mains electricity is dangerous.

## Still offline?

- **Owners and admins:** **Diagnostics** shows each board, when it was last seen and what to try. Press **See what's wrong** on the Home banner to jump there.
- Tell your installer: in the phone app, **Me** → **Message your expert**. In the web app, **Diagnostics** → **Send to support**. Your installer is also told automatically when a board goes offline.
- Members and guests: tell the home owner.

When the board is back, you get "… is back online" and its devices can be switched again.
