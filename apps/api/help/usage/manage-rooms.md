Rooms group your devices, and access for members and guests is given room by room. Your installer usually sets up the rooms on installation day. Owners and admins can change them at any time in the web app.

## Add a room

1. Open **Rooms**.
2. Press **Add a room**, type a **Name** such as "Kids room", and save.

Each room name can be used only once in a home ("A room with that name already exists.").

## Rename or delete a room

1. In **Rooms**, open the **⋯** menu on the room's tile.
2. Choose **Rename** or **Delete room**.

When you delete a room, its devices are kept. They move to **Devices without a room**, which only owners and admins can see. Members and guests lose access to them until you place them in another room.

## Move a device to a room

Under **Devices without a room**, use **Choose a room…** next to the device to place it.

To move a device that's already in a room to a different one, or to rename a device, ask your installer. They can do it from the board's **Devices & buttons** page.

## After changing rooms

- Check **People**. A new room starts as **No access** for every member and guest, so give access where needed.
- The phone app follows the same rooms and shows them in the **Rooms** tab.
- In the phone app, a room has **All on** and **All off** for the devices you're allowed to switch.

The phone app can't add or change rooms. Use the web app at https://home.fawadiqbal.dev.
