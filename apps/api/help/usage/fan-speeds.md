A ceiling fan on a smart regulator has speeds, not just on and off.

- **Web app:** tap the fan's tile to switch it on or off; tap **1**, **2**, **3** or **4** under it to choose a speed.
- **Phone app:** open the fan and choose a speed under **Speed**. Tapping its tile switches it on or off.
- **Assistant:** "Bedroom fan speed 2", "Pankha tez karo", "Fan dheema kar do".

The fan remembers its speed: switching it on again brings back the speed it had. A wall switch on the fan's board switches it on and off at that speed.
