Switchboard has a light and a dark look. The first time you open it, it follows your phone's or computer's own setting. After that, it remembers your choice.

## In the web app

- **On a computer:** at the bottom of the left sidebar, next to your name, press the moon icon to switch to dark, or the sun icon to switch back to light.
- **On a phone browser:** tap the home's name at the top of the screen, then choose **Dark mode** or **Light mode**.
- The sign-in page has the same button at the top.

Your choice is saved in that browser only. Another browser or device keeps its own setting.

## In the phone app

1. Go to the **Me** tab.
2. Tap **Appearance**.
3. Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Like my phone**. **Like my phone** follows your phone's setting automatically, including any schedule your phone uses.

## If it keeps changing back

- In the web app, private or incognito windows don't keep settings. Open Switchboard in a normal window.
- In the phone app, if **Like my phone** is selected, the look changes whenever your phone switches between light and dark. Pick **Light** or **Dark** to keep it fixed.
