If your home has a maintenance plan with your installer's company, the owner and admins can see it in the web app under **Plan & bills**:

- **Your plan:** its name, the price each month, quarter or year, the visits included, and when the next bill is made.
- **Bills:** what's due, what's overdue, and what's paid. Open a bill to see it in full. From there you can **Print or save as PDF**, or **Send on WhatsApp**.

You get a notification (and an email, if your company has email set up) when a new bill is made, when your payment is recorded, and if a bill is still unpaid a day after its due date.

## Paying

Pay the way you agreed with your installer's company: cash, bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. The app doesn't take payments. The team records your payment, and the bill then shows **Paid**. If you've paid but it still shows due, send a help request with the transaction ID.

You can also ask the assistant: "what's my bill?", "is anything due?". This is your plan with the company. For your electricity bill estimate, ask "how much electricity did we use?".
