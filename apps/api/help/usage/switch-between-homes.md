If you belong to more than one home, for example your own and your parents', you use the same account for all of them and switch between them. Each home keeps its own rooms, devices, people and timers.

## In the web app

1. Click the card at the top of the left sidebar. It shows the current home's name and your role there.
2. Under **Homes**, pick the home you want.

On a phone browser, tap the home's name at the top of the screen and pick from the list.

## In the phone app

- **Me** → **Switch home** (it shows how many homes you have), then pick one.
- Or on the **Home** tab, tap the home's name and choose from **Your homes**.

## Good to know

- Switchboard opens the home you used last time.
- Your role can differ per home. You may be the owner of one and a member of another.
- The assistant keeps a separate conversation in each home, and only controls the home that's open. "Turn off the fan" always means the fan in the home you're looking at.
- Each home sends its own notifications.
- To be added to another home, ask that home's owner for an invite link. If you already have an account, you just confirm your password when you open the link.

## If a home is missing from the list

Your access to that home may have been removed, or its invite was never accepted. Ask the home's owner to invite you again.
