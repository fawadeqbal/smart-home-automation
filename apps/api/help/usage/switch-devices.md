You can switch any device you have access to in three ways: in the web app, in the phone app, or with the wall button next to it.

## In the web app

1. Open **Home**. Devices are grouped by room.
2. Tap a tile. It turns amber when the device is on, and a spinner shows while the board confirms the change (about a second).
3. Under each tile, **History** shows when it was switched.

A grey tile that says **Offline** can't be switched from the app right now. Its wall button still works.

## In the phone app

1. On **Home** or in **Rooms**, tap a tile to switch it.
2. Tap the arrow on the tile, or long-press it, to open its sheet. You get a big switch, **Turn off automatically** (see the timers article), phrases to say to the assistant, and its recent history.
3. Tap the star to put a device in **Favourites** on the Home tab.
4. A room has **All on** and **All off**. The Home tab has **All off** with **Undo**.

## With your voice

Open the assistant and say or type, for example, "turn off the bedroom fan" or "batti band karo".

## At the wall

If your installer fitted a wall button for a device, press it once to toggle the device on or off. The board handles the button by itself, so it works even when the internet or Wi-Fi is down. The app shows "wall switch" next to a device last switched that way.

## What you can and can't switch

- You can only switch devices in rooms where you have **Can switch** access. With **Can see**, the tile says **view only**.
- Sensors (temperature, doors, leaks) show readings. They can't be switched.
- Switching is on or off only. There is no dimming, fan speed or AC temperature.

## If a switch doesn't work

If the app says a device **did not respond** or is **Not responding**, see "A device doesn't respond or the switch isn't confirmed".
