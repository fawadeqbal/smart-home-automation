Owners and admins invite people. You choose a role and, room by room, what they may do. Switchboard doesn't send emails or texts: you get a link to send, usually on WhatsApp.

## In the web app

1. Open **People** and press **Invite someone**.
2. Enter their **Email**. This is required: it becomes their sign-in. You can also add a **Name**.
3. Choose a role:
   - **Member**: family who use only the rooms you share.
   - **Guest**: someone staying for a while.
   - **Home admin** (owners only): like the owner, except they can't change the owner or other admins.
4. For a member or guest, set each room to **No access**, **Can see**, **Can switch** or, for members only, **Switch & rename**.
5. Press **Create invite link**, copy the link and send it to them.

## In the phone app

1. **Me** → **Family** → **Invite someone**.
2. Enter a name and email, then pick **Family** or **Guest**.
3. Under **Can use**, keep **All rooms** or pick rooms. People invited from the phone get **Can switch** in those rooms. Use the web app if you want **Can see** only.
4. Tap **Make an invite link**, then **Send the link** (opens WhatsApp or another app) or **Copy link**.

## What happens next

- The person opens the link, chooses a password and joins. If they already have a Switchboard account with that email, they confirm their password instead.
- The link works **once** and for **7 days**. Pending invites appear in **People** with their end date and a **Cancel** button.
- Making a new invite for the same email replaces the old link.

## Guests

Guests can switch things in the rooms you pick, but can never get **Switch & rename**. Remove guests from **People** when they leave, or give them an end date so their access stops by itself.

## For a while, or at certain hours

A member or guest can have an **end date** (a guest for the weekend) and/or **hours** on some days (the maid from 9:00 to 17:00, Monday to Saturday; a night guard from 22:00 to 06:00, which goes past midnight). Times are in your home's time zone.

- **When inviting:** tick **For a while, or at certain hours** (web) or switch it on (phone), then pick an end (Tonight, Tomorrow, The weekend, A week, or a date) and/or the hours and days.
- **Later:** web **People** → **⋯** → **Change access hours**; phone **Me** → **Family** → **Hours**.
- **Assistant:** "Invite Shazia as a guest from 9 to 5, Monday to Saturday", "Let Bilal in until Sunday 8 pm", "Extend Bilal until next Friday".

Outside their hours they can still open the app, but they can't see or switch anything: the app says "Your access to <home> is from 9:00 to 17:00". Their phone's Wi‑Fi passes stop working then too. After the end date the People page shows them as **Expired** and you're told; press **Extend** to let them in again, or remove them.

## If it doesn't work

- "This person is already in this home." They're already a member. Change their access instead.
- "Only the owner can invite an admin." Admins can invite members and guests only.
- If the link won't open for them, see "My invite link doesn't work".
