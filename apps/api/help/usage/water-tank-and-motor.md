If your installer fitted a tank sensor, the app shows how full your water tank is, and can look after the water motor.

## See the level

- **Web app:** the tank's tile shows the level in % with a bar.
- **Phone app:** the tank's tile, or open it for the big reading.
- **Assistant:** ask "How full is the tank?" or "Tanki mein kitna pani hai?"

## The motor looks after itself

- **Stops when full.** When the tank reaches the "stop" level (95% unless you change it), the motor is switched off. If the motor is on the same board as the tank sensor, the board does this itself, even when the internet is down.
- **Stops if something is wrong.** If the level isn't rising a few minutes after the motor starts (no water in the main line, a dry well, a closed valve), or the motor runs much longer than it should, it is switched off and you get a notice. It then won't start by itself until someone switches the motor on again.
- **Starts when low (optional).** Owners and admins can choose a level to start the motor by itself: open the tank → **Water motor** → **Start the motor by itself**. It is off until you choose a level.

## Notices

You are told when the motor was stopped because something looked wrong. "The tank is full" and "running low" notices are off unless you turn them on under **Notifications** settings.
