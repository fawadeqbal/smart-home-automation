Switchboard can tell who's home, and switch things off when everyone has left.

## Share when you're home (each person, in the phone app)

**Me** → **Share when I'm home**. It's off until you turn it on, for each home.

Your phone then tells the home whether it's on your home's Wi‑Fi (it checks whether it can reach one of your boards). No location is used. It checks when you open the app, when your phone changes network, and every few minutes while the app is open.

The owner and admins see who's home on the Home screen; so do the others who share it.

## When the last person leaves (owners and admins)

**Web:** **Automations** → **When everyone leaves** → **Set up**. **Phone:** **Routines** → **When everyone leaves**.

- **Switch off lights and fans** (anything called a light, bulb, batti, lamp or fan; never the fridge or the router), or **chosen devices** (web), or nothing.
- **Turn on away mode**: doors and motion alert at any hour.
- **Tell me**.
- **When the first person comes home:** run a scene (like "Welcome"); away mode goes off again if this turned it on.

Only people who share when they're home count, and it waits until **all** of them are out. Someone the home hasn't heard from (their phone is off) counts as out after 20 minutes, so a flaky reading doesn't switch your lights off. It runs with the rights of the person who set it up: it never switches what they couldn't, and respects child lock like other automations.

## If it doesn't work

- Nobody counts: each person must turn on **Share when I'm home** in the phone app.
- It thinks you're out while you're home: your phone must be on the same Wi‑Fi as the boards, and the app must be opened now and then (phones stop apps in the background).
