## Two-step sign-in

With two-step sign-in on, signing in needs your password **and** a 6-digit code from an app on your phone. Someone who learns your password still can't get in. Super admins should always have it on.

**Turn it on (web app)**
1. Click your name at the bottom of the menu to open **Your account**.
2. Under **Two-step sign-in**, press **Turn on**.
3. Open an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy, all free). Add an account and scan the square code. If you can't scan it, type the key shown under it.
4. Type the 6-digit code the app shows, and press **Turn on**.
5. **Save your recovery codes.** Copy or download them and keep them somewhere safe. Each one lets you sign in once without your phone.

**Signing in:** after your email and password, type the code from the app. Each code works once and changes every 30 seconds. If it's refused, check that your phone sets its time automatically, and use the newest code. The phone app asks for it too.

**Lost your phone?** On the code step, press **No phone? Use a recovery code**. Then turn two-step sign-in off and on again with your new phone. Lost the codes as well? Your company's super admin can turn it off for you (**Team** → **⋯** → **Turn off two-step sign-in**). You get an email when that happens.

**New recovery codes** or **Turn off**: on **Your account**, with your password.

## Where you're signed in

**Your account** → **Where you're signed in** lists each browser and phone signed in to your account, when it was last used and from which internet address.

- **Sign out** ends one of them. **Sign out everywhere else** ends all but the one you're using.
- Something you don't recognise? Sign it out, then change your password. Changing your password signs out every other device by itself.
