The assistant switches things, sets timers, runs scenes and answers questions about your home. You can speak or type in English, in Urdu, or in a mix of both.

## Open it

- **Web app:** press the round **Ask** button (it's on every page), or use the ask bar on **Home**. Press **Tap to talk**, speak, then tap again to send. Recording stops by itself after 15 seconds. You can also type and press Enter.
- **Phone app:** tap the microphone button to open **Ask Switchboard**. Tap **Speak**, talk, then tap **Stop and send**. Or use **Type instead**. Turn **Read answers aloud** on or off as you like.

## Things to try

| English | Roman Urdu |
|---|---|
| Turn on the bedroom light | Bedroom ki batti jala do |
| Switch off everything in the kitchen | Kitchen ki sab cheezen band karo |
| Turn on the fan for 10 minutes | Pankha 10 minute ke liye chala do |
| Turn off the bulb in half an hour | Bulb aadhe ghante baad band kar do |
| Every day at 11 pm turn off the lights | Roz raat 11 baje battiyan band kar do |
| What's on right now? | Kya kya on hai? |
| What is the temperature in the living room? | Living room mein temperature kitna hai? |
| Is the front door open? | Kya front door khula hai? |
| Good night (a scene's name) | — |
| Cancel all timers | Sab timer cancel karo |

"It", "that one" and "also the fan" refer to what you said just before. If two devices match, for example two lights, it asks which one you mean.

## How it answers

- It replies in the language you used. Short everyday commands ("bulb off", "batti band karo") are handled instantly and confirmed in English, for example "Done, the bulb is off."
- Answers in Urdu script are read aloud in a natural Urdu voice. English names in them, like "Bedroom" or "AC", are said the way you'd say them in Urdu. Answers in English or Roman Urdu use the English voice.
- It only reports something as done after the board confirms it.
- It can only do what **you** may do. Rooms you can only see are view-only for the assistant too.
- In the web app, **New conversation** starts afresh. A conversation also starts afresh by itself after about 30 minutes of quiet.

## Asking it to remember things

Say "remember that…" and the assistant keeps it for your later chats in this home. For example: "remember that the store light is the one by the stairs", or "yaad rakhna Ali ka kamra upar wala bedroom hai". After that, "turn on the stairs light" switches the store light.

- Only you see what it remembers for you. Other people in the home don't.
- To see the list, or remove something, open **Your account** (click your name in the web app), under **What the assistant remembers**. You can also say "forget that the store light…".
- It never stores passwords or card numbers, even if you ask.

## What it can't do

- Send messages, call people, or set reminders or alarms that don't switch a device.
- Change Wi-Fi, or add or rewire devices. Your installer does that.
- Dim lights, change fan speed or set an AC's temperature. Devices are on/off.
- Control anything outside your home, or tell you news, outside weather or prices. It has no internet access.
