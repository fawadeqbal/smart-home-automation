Switchboard estimates the electricity your switches use and what it costs. It knows how long each switch was on. It multiplies that by how much power the device draws (its watts) and by the price of one unit (kWh).

**It's an estimate, not a meter.** Your electricity meter and bill are the real numbers. Devices that aren't on a Switchboard switch (a fridge on a normal socket, for example) aren't counted.

## Where to see it

- **Web app:** **Electricity** in the menu. Pick **Today**, **7 days** or **30 days**. You see the units used, the cost, a month at this pace, units per day and each device's share.
- **Home page** (web and phone): a card with this week's units and rupees. On the phone, tap it to see which devices used the most.
- **Ask the assistant:** "how much electricity did we use this week?", "which device uses the most?", "bijli ka bill kitna aayega?"

## Make it more accurate (owners and admins)

- **Price of one unit:** on the Electricity page, press **Set your rate** under Cost. To find your rate, divide the total on your bill by the units used. This way taxes are included. Until you set it, a typical rate (Rs 60 a unit) is used.
- **Power of each device:** press **Set power** next to a device and enter its watts. You can find them on its label or box. Without a value, the power is guessed from the device's name: a fan about 75 W, an LED light 15 W, an AC 1500 W, a geyser 2000 W, an iron 1000 W, a water motor 750 W. A switch whose name says nothing (like "Socket 2") counts as 0 until you set it.

## Good to know

- Power cuts are left out, because nothing runs without power.
- An AC or a fridge doesn't draw full power all the time (the compressor turns on and off). The estimate for those may come out higher than the real use.
- Members and guests see only the switches in the rooms they have access to.
