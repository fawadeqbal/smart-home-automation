Timers run once. Schedules repeat every day, or on the days you pick. Both run on the Switchboard server, so they work even when your phone is off or the app is closed.

## By voice (quickest)

- "Turn on the fan for 10 minutes": it goes on now and off after 10 minutes.
- "Turn off the bulb in half an hour" or "pankha 5 minute baad band kar do"
- "Turn on the porch light at 7:30 pm". If that time has already passed today, it runs tomorrow.
- "Every day at 11 pm turn off the lights" or "on weekdays at 6 am turn on the geyser"
- "What timers are set?" and "cancel the bulb timer" or "cancel all timers"

## In the web app

1. Open **Automations** and, under **Timers & schedules**, press **New**.
2. **1 · What should happen?** Choose **Switch devices** and set each device on or off, or choose **Run a scene**.
3. **2 · When?** Choose **After a while** (1 to 1440 minutes), **At a time** (today, or tomorrow if that time has passed) or **Every day**. With **Every day** you can tick days. If you tick none, it runs every day.
4. You can add a **Name (optional)**, then press **Save**.

Use the switch next to a schedule to pause it, and **Resume** to start it again.

## In the phone app

- **Quick auto-off:** open a device's sheet while it is on, and under **Turn off automatically** pick 15 min, 30 min, 1 h or 2 h. Tap the same choice again to cancel it.
- **Routines** tab → **New routine**: pick **Turn on** or **Turn off**, then **In a while**, **Once at** (use **Other…** to choose any time) or **Repeat** on chosen days. Press **Save routine**.
- To delete a schedule, long-press it and confirm.

## Good to know

- Times use your home's time zone (normally Pakistan time).
- A timer or schedule acts with **your** rights. If your access to that room is removed, it stops working there.
- Members and guests see only their own timers. Owners and admins see everyone's.
- One timer or schedule can switch at most 30 devices.

## If a timer didn't run

If the board was offline at that moment, or the device didn't respond, the timer fails. You get a notification saying it "did not fully work". It does **not** try again later. Check the board (see "A board is offline"), then set the timer again.
