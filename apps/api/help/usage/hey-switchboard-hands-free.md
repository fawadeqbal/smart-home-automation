With hands-free on, you say **"Hey Switchboard"** and then what you want, for example "Hey Switchboard, turn off the kitchen light". You don't need to touch the screen. The answer is spoken and shown in a card over whatever screen is open.

## Privacy: what is heard where

The wake word is recognised **on your own phone or computer**. Nothing goes to Switchboard until it hears "Hey Switchboard". After that, only the request you say next is sent to the Switchboard server for speech recognition, the same as when you tap the microphone.

## Turn it on in the web app

1. Open the assistant with the **Ask** button.
2. Turn on the **"Hey Switchboard"** switch ("Talk without touching the screen").
3. Allow the microphone when the browser asks.
4. When it says **Say "Hey Switchboard" any time**, it's listening. A small marker on the **Ask** button shows it's ready.

The browser remembers your choice. After a reload it may show **Tap anywhere to start listening**, because browsers only open the microphone after a tap. It needs https and a recent browser.

## Turn it on in the phone app

1. Go to **Me** → **HANDS-FREE** and turn on **"Hey Switchboard"**.
2. Allow the microphone.
3. It listens while the app is open ("Say "Hey Switchboard" any time the app is open").

### Android: listening in the background

With hands-free on, Android also shows **Keep listening in the background**. Turn it on to use the wake word with the screen off or another app open.

- Android shows a permanent notification, **Listening for "Hey Switchboard"**, with a **Stop** button. Android requires this notification.
- It **uses more battery**. Turn it off when you don't need it.
- Swiping the app away stops it. It doesn't start by itself after the phone restarts. Open the app and it starts again.
- Background listening is Android only.

## Using it

- After the chime, say your request in English or Urdu.
- If the assistant asks you something back ("Which light?"), just answer. You don't need the wake word again.
- Say "Hey Switchboard" while it's talking to interrupt it. The **×** on the card stops the current exchange.
- Tapping the microphone on the **Ask** screen pauses hands-free while you record.

## If you don't see the switch

The switch only appears when your app version and browser support hands-free. Otherwise, tap the microphone instead. For other problems, see "The assistant can't hear me".
