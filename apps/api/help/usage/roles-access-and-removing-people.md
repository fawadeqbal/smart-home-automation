## The four roles in a home

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Home owner** | Everything in the home: switch, scenes, timers, voice, rooms, invite people and set their access. There is exactly one owner. |
| **Home admin** | Like the owner, except they can't change the owner or other admins, or make new admins. |
| **Member** | Only the rooms shared with them, at the level you choose per room. |
| **Guest** | Like a member, but never more than **Can switch**. |

Owners and admins see every room. Wiring, boards, pins and firmware are handled by your installer in every role.

## Room access levels (members and guests)

- **No access**: the room and its devices are hidden.
- **Can see**: they see the devices and readings, marked **view only**, but can't switch them. The assistant won't switch them either.
- **Can switch**: they can switch devices and include them in their own timers and scenes.
- **Switch & rename**: members only. The same switching rights, plus permission to rename devices in that room.

Devices that aren't in any room are visible to owners and admins only.

## Change someone's access (web app)

1. Open **People**. The table has one column per room.
2. For a member or guest, pick the new level in a room's cell. It applies straight away.
3. To change a role, use the **Role** dropdown. Owners can choose Admin, Member or Guest. Admins can choose Member or Guest.

## Remove or disable someone

In **People**, open the **⋯** menu on the person's row:
- **Remove from home**: they can no longer open this home. If they belong to no other home, their account is deleted, and so are the timers and scenes they created.
- **Disable account**: blocks sign-in and signs them out everywhere. This option only works for someone who belongs to no other home.

You can't remove the owner or change your own account here. Admins can't change owners or other admins.

## Timers and schedules after a change

Timers and schedules run with their creator's rights. If you remove someone's access to a room, their schedules there stop working.

## Changing the owner

The app has no button to hand ownership to someone else. Ask your installer.
