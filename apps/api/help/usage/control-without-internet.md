When your home's internet stops working, your wall switches still work as always. And if your Wi‑Fi router is still on, the **phone app** can switch things straight on your boards, without the internet.

## How to use it

1. Make sure your phone is connected to **your home's Wi‑Fi** (not mobile data alone).
2. Open the Switchboard app. If it can't reach the internet, it offers **Control over home Wi‑Fi**. You can also find it any time under **Me → Control over home Wi‑Fi**.
3. Switch lights, fans and sockets as usual. Sensors show their latest reading.

Pull down on the page to check the boards again. Tap **Try the internet again** when the internet is back.

## Before you need it

- Open the app **once with internet** on each phone that should be able to do this. The app saves what it needs and keeps it up to date by itself.
- Your boards need firmware **2.5.0 or newer**. Your installer can see this on each board's page in the web app ("Without internet"), and updates boards over Wi‑Fi.

## What works and what doesn't

| Works without internet | Needs the internet |
|---|---|
| Switching lights, fans, sockets from the phone | Timers, schedules, scenes and rules |
| Sensor readings in the app | Alerts and notifications |
| Wall switches and buttons (always) | The assistant, voice and "Hey Switchboard" |
| | The web app, and phones away from home |

When the internet comes back, the boards tell the server everything that changed, and your activity history fills in.

## Who can do what

The phone can only do what that person may do: the same rooms, and view-only rooms stay view-only. Child lock still applies. When the owner removes someone or reduces their rights, that phone stops working for the boards as soon as they're online again.

## If it doesn't work

- **"Can't reach … Is this phone on the home's Wi‑Fi?"** Connect to the home's Wi‑Fi, and check that the router is on. Mobile data can't reach your boards.
- **"Needs firmware 2.5.0 or newer"** Ask your installer to update that board.
- **"Doesn't accept this phone's pass yet"** Open the app once with the internet on, then try again.
- **The option doesn't appear** The app was never opened with internet on this phone, or you signed out. Sign in once with internet.
- The web app in a browser can't do this. Use the phone app.
