You join Switchboard through an **invite link**. Your installer sends it to the home owner, and the owner sends links to the rest of the family. Nobody signs up on their own.

## Accept your invite

1. Open the link you received (usually on WhatsApp) in your phone's or computer's browser.
2. The page shows the home you are joining, its rooms and who set it up.
3. Type your name and choose a password. It needs **at least 8 characters**, **a number or a symbol**, and must not be a common password such as "pakistan123". The screen ticks each rule off as you type.
4. Press **Open my home** (owners and admins) or **Open** followed by the home's name (family and guests).

If you already have a Switchboard account with that email, you only confirm your existing password.

The link works **once** and for **7 days**.

## Signing in later

Go to **https://home.fawadiqbal.dev**, enter your email and password and press **Sign in**. You stay signed in on that browser or phone. If you don't open Switchboard for 30 days, you sign in again.

## The first tour (owners)

The first time an owner opens their home, a short tour shows the rooms, lets you switch a real light, invite family, pick your alerts and add the app to your home screen. Press **Skip for now** if you'd rather do it later.

## Finding your way around

- **Home** shows a greeting, how many things are on, your scenes, and every room with its devices as tiles. Tap a tile to switch it.
- **Automations** holds timers, daily schedules and scenes.
- Owners and admins also see **Rooms**, **Diagnostics**, **People** and **Activity**.
- The bell at the top shows notifications.
- The round **Ask** button opens the assistant.

In the phone app the tabs are **Home**, **Rooms**, **Routines** and **Me**, plus the microphone button for **Ask Switchboard**.

## If you see "You're not part of a home yet"

Your account isn't in a home. Ask the home owner, or your installer, to send you an invite link.
