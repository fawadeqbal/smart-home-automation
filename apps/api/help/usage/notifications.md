Switchboard tells you about things worth knowing: a water leak, a door opened at night, a board going offline. Notifications appear under the bell at the top of the web app and are kept for 30 days.

## What you can be told

| Alert | On unless you turn it off? |
|---|---|
| Water leak detected | Yes |
| Door or window opened at night (10 pm – 6 am) | Yes |
| Motion at night (10 pm – 6 am) | No: off unless you turn it on |
| Very hot or cold room (above 40 °C or below 5 °C) | Yes |
| One of my timers or schedules failed | Yes |
| A board disconnected or came back (owners and admins) | Yes |
| A board has low voltage or weak Wi-Fi (owners and admins) | Yes |

You only get sensor alerts for rooms you can see. You need the matching sensor fitted: no leak sensor, no leak alerts.

## Choose your alerts

- **Web app:** click the bell → **All notifications & settings**. Under **Tell me when…**, switch each alert on or off.
- **Phone app:** **Me** → **NOTIFY ME WHEN**: **Something stops working**, **Water, doors and heat**, **Movement at night**.

## Get alerts when the app is closed

This works through the web app:
1. On the phone or computer you want alerts on, open the web app and go to **All notifications & settings**.
2. Press **Turn on alerts for this device** and allow notifications.
3. **On iPhone:** first add the app to the Home Screen (Share → **Add to Home Screen**), open it from there, then press the button.

Do this on each device you want alerts on. The page shows how many devices have alerts turned on.

The Switchboard phone app saves your alert choices but doesn't show or deliver the alerts itself. Use the web app (in the browser or added to your home screen) to receive them.

## Reading and clearing

Press **Open** on a notification to go to the device or board it's about. Use **Read**, or **Mark all read**.
