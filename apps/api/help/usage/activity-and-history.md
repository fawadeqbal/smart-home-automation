Switchboard records every switch and every change to your home, so you can see what happened, when and how.

## One device's history (everyone)

- **Web app:** under a device's tile, press **History**. You see each change with **When**, **State** and **Cause**:
  - **App or voice**: switched from the app, the assistant or a schedule
  - **Wall button**: pressed on the wall
  - **Board restarted**: the state the board came back with after power returned
  - **Reading**: a sensor reading
  
  Sensors also show a small chart with the low and high values.
- **Phone app:** open the device's sheet. **Lately** lists its recent changes.

You can see the history of devices in rooms shared with you.

## Everything that was switched (phone app)

**Me** → **Activity** lists switching across the home, newest first: who did it, and whether it was from the app, by voice, on the wall or by a schedule. Filter it with **People**, **Schedules** or **Wall**. The **Home** tab shows the latest under **Just now**. Tap **All activity** to see more.

## Changes to the home (owners and admins, web app)

**Activity** shows changes to the home made by you, your family and your installer: people invited or removed, room access changed, devices added or renamed, boards going offline and coming back, timers created. Each row shows **When**, **Who**, **Action** and **What**. You can filter by **Access**, **Devices** or **System**.

## Good to know

- If wall buttons are pressed while a board is offline, the board reports where each device ended up once it reconnects. The individual presses aren't listed.
- Temperature readings appear in the device's own history, not in the switching list.
- Notifications are a separate list and are kept for 30 days.
