## Rules: "when this happens, do that"

A rule waits for something to happen in the home and then acts. For example:

- When the **gate opens** between 10 pm and 6 am, turn the **porch light** on for 5 minutes and tell me.
- When **motion** is seen in the store room while you're away, tell everyone.
- When the bedroom gets **above 32 °C**, turn the fan on.
- When the **water sensor** finds water, turn the motor off.

**By voice:** say it as you would to a person: "when the gate opens at night, turn on the porch light for 5 minutes".

**In the web app:** open **Automations** and, under **Rules**, press **New rule**.

1. **When…** pick the device and what it does: a switch turns on or off; a door or window opens or closes; a motion sensor sees movement; a water sensor finds water; or a temperature or level goes above or below a number.
2. **Only…** (optional): between two times, or only while the home is in away mode.
3. **Then…** switch devices (optionally "switch back off after" some minutes), run a scene, and/or send a notice.

A rule runs at most once every 2 minutes, so a door opening and closing again doesn't flood you. Use the switch next to a rule to pause it. In the phone app, rules are under **Routines**: you can pause them there, and long-press to delete.

A rule acts with the rights of the person who made it. Anyone can make rules for the devices they're allowed to switch. Only the owner and admins can send a notice to other people.

## Away mode

Turn it on from the home page (web or phone), or say "turn on away mode". While it is on:

- Any door, window or motion sensor sends an **alert to everyone** at any hour, not only at night.
- If the owner picked lights (press **Lights** on the away mode card), they go on and off at random from just after sunset until 11:30 pm, so the home looks lived-in.

Press **I'm home** (or turn the switch off) when you're back. Everyone in the home is told when away mode is turned on or off.

## Child lock

The owner or an admin opens a device and turns on **Child lock**. After that, only the owner and admins can switch it from the app, the assistant or a rule. The wall switch still works. It's good for an iron, a heater or a water motor.

## Switch off by itself (auto-off)

On a device's page, under **Safety**, the owner or an admin picks **Switch off by itself**: after 15 minutes up to 4 hours. Whoever turns it on (app, voice, wall switch or schedule), it goes off that long afterwards. Use it for a geyser, an iron, a motor or a heater, so nothing is left running by mistake.

## Good to know

- Rules, away mode and auto-off run on the Switchboard server. They need the board to be online. If the internet is down, the wall switches still work, but rules can't act.
- If a rule's device doesn't respond, the rule shows "Failed" under **Last ran**. It doesn't try again.
