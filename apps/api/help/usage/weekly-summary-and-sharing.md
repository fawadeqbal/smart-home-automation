The summary tells you how your home did in a few lines:

- **Power:** power cuts, and how long you were without power.
- **Internet:** times it went down, if any.
- **Electricity:** about how many units you used and what they cost, and which device used the most (see "How much electricity is my home using?").
- **Automations:** how many times timers, rules and away mode switched something.
- **Alerts:** water leaks, doors opened at night, away-mode alerts.
- **Boards:** whether they're all online.

## Where to see it

- **Web app:** on the home page, press **This week in short** next to the greeting. Choose **Today** or **This week**.
- **Phone app:** tap the electricity card on the Home tab. The week's summary is at the top.
- **Assistant:** "how was this week?", "give me today's summary".

## Send it to the family

- **Web app:** press **Send on WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with the summary already typed; pick who to send it to.
- **Phone app:** press **Send to the family** and choose WhatsApp (or any other app).
- The Electricity page also has a **Share on WhatsApp** button.

## Sent to you automatically

- Owners and admins get the **weekly summary every Sunday evening** as a notification. If email is set up for your company, it is also emailed to them.
- A **daily summary** at 9 pm is off by default. Turn it on in **Notifications**, under **Tell me when…** ("A summary of the day"). You can also turn the weekly one, or its email, off there.
- Members and guests can open the summary any time. It counts only the rooms they have access to.
