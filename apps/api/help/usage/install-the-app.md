You can use Switchboard in two ways on a phone: the **Switchboard phone app**, or the **web app added to your home screen**. Both use the same account, rooms and rights, and both update live.

## Option 1: Add the web app to your home screen (Android and iPhone)

This opens like an app, and there's nothing to download from a store.

- **Android (Chrome):** open https://home.fawadiqbal.dev and sign in. Open the browser menu (⋮) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. On a computer, an **Install app** button may also appear in the left sidebar.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open https://home.fawadiqbal.dev, tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

On iPhone, this is also how you get alerts when the app is closed (see "Choose your alerts").

## Option 2: The Switchboard phone app

1. Install the app. Ask your installer how to get it for your phone.
2. On first start it asks for your **Server address**. Enter **https://home.fawadiqbal.dev** and press **Continue**.
3. Sign in with your email and password.

The app has four tabs (**Home**, **Rooms**, **Routines** and **Me**) and a microphone button for **Ask Switchboard**. On Android it can also keep listening for "Hey Switchboard" in the background.

New to Switchboard? Accept your invite link in the browser first, then sign in on the app.

## Which should I use?

- **Phone app:** quick tiles, favourites, auto-off timers in each device's sheet, and hands-free voice with background listening on Android.
- **Web app:** everything the phone app does, plus **Automations** for scenes and detailed schedules, **People** for room-by-room access, **Rooms**, **Activity**, **Diagnostics**, and alerts delivered when the app is closed.

Many people use the phone app day to day and the web app for setting things up.

## If it doesn't work

- "Can't find Switchboard at …" means the server address is wrong, or the phone is offline. Check the address. On the sign-in screen, tap **Change** next to **Server**.
- For voice in the browser, the address must start with **https://**.
