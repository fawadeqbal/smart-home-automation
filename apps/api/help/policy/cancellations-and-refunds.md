## Cancelling a free visit

The first visit is free. Cancel or move it at any time by replying to our call or WhatsApp. There's no charge.

## Advance for an installation

When you accept our price, we ask for an **advance of 50% [[check: advance percentage]]** to book your installation day and set aside your boards. The rest is paid **when the installation is finished and tested [[check: balance due on completion]]**.

## Cancelling before installation day

| When you cancel | Refund of the advance |
|---|---|
| More than 48 hours before installation day | Full refund [[check: full refund window]] |
| Less than 48 hours before | Advance minus Rs 1,500 [[check: late cancellation deduction]] |
| Parts bought specially for your home (for example extra sensors) | Their cost may be kept [[check: special-order parts non-refundable]] |

## After installation

- **Within 7 days [[check: return window]]** of installation, if you're not happy, we remove the boards and restore your switchboards as they were. We refund the hardware price **minus the installation labour [[check: refund after installation, what is deducted]]**.
- After that, faults are handled under the warranty (see "Warranty on boards and installation work"), not by refund.
- Charged service visits are not refundable once the work is done **[[check: service visits non-refundable]]**.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back by **bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa [[check: refund methods]]**, within **10 working days [[check: refund time]]** of the cancellation or the removal of the boards.

## Your account after cancelling

If you stop using Switchboard, we remove your home from our system **on request [[check: account closure process]]**. See "Privacy and your data". Boards we take back are reset and leave your home's account.

## How to ask

Message us from the app (**Me** → **Message your expert**) or call us **[[check: phone number for cancellations]]**.
