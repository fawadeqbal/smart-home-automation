## Our hours

**Monday to Saturday, 10:00 am to 7:00 pm** Pakistan time [[check: support days and hours]]. Closed on **Sundays and public holidays** [[check: closed days]].

You can send a message from the app at any time: **Me** → **Message your expert**, or **Diagnostics** → **Send to support**. Messages sent outside our hours are answered on the next working day.

## Always on (automatic)

Switchboard watches your boards around the clock. When a board goes offline or reports a problem, you and your installer get a notification automatically, at any hour. A person looks at it during our hours **[[check: website says "Looked after 24/7" — decide whether staff respond out of hours]]**.

## Response times

| What | When we respond |
|---|---|
| New visit request from the website | A call within **1 working day** [[check: matches website promise]] |
| Message from the app | A first reply within **4 working hours** [[check: first reply time]] |
| Whole home offline, or a safety concern | Same working day [[check: urgent response time]] |
| Problem that needs a visit | Visit within **2 working days** in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad [[check: visit lead time]] |
| Remote fixes (Wi-Fi move, restart, firmware, settings) | Usually during the first reply [[check: remote fix expectation]] |

We reply by phone or WhatsApp **[[check: reply channels]]**.

## Emergencies

If you see sparks or smoke, smell burning, or a switchboard feels hot, **switch off the breaker for that circuit** first and call an electrician if needed. Don't wait for us.

Everything else can wait safely: the wall buttons keep working even when a board is offline or the internet is down.
