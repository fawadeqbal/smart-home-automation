## The first visit is free

Booking through **Book a free visit** gets you a home visit and a clear price, **free and with no obligation [[check: matches website promise]]**. We don't start any work or charge anything until you accept the price.

## What's free after installation

- **Remote help**: anything we can do from our console, such as moving a board to new Wi-Fi while it's still online, restarting a board, firmware updates, fixing device names or rooms, and resending invites **[[check: remote help always free]]**.
- **Visits for problems covered by the warranty** (see "Warranty on boards and installation work").

## Charged visits

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Service visit within city limits (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad) | Rs 1,500 [[check: visit fee]] |
| Visit outside city limits | Rs 1,500 plus travel, quoted beforehand [[check: out-of-city travel charge]] |
| Labour after the first hour | Rs 1,000 per hour [[check: hourly labour rate]] |
| Replacement board, out of warranty | Quoted at the time [[check: board replacement price]] |
| Adding a new board, device, sensor or wall button | Quoted before the work [[check: add-on pricing]] |
| Moving boards to new Wi-Fi on site (router changed and boards offline) | Normal visit fee [[check: wifi re-setup on site charged]] |

We tell you the price **before** any charged work starts. If a visit shows the problem is covered by the warranty, the visit is free **[[check: visit fee waived if warranty]]**.

## Booking a visit

Ask from the app: **Me** → **Message your expert**, or **Diagnostics** → **Send to support**. We aim to visit within **2 working days** in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad **[[check: visit lead time and cities]]**. See "Support hours and response times".

## Payment

Pay the installer or our office by **cash, bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa [[check: payment methods]]**. Payment is due **when the work is done [[check: payment timing]]**. New installations need an advance, as described in "Cancellations and refunds". Switchboard doesn't take payments inside the app.

## Missed visits

If nobody is home at the agreed time and you didn't tell us at least **2 hours [[check: notice period]]** before, we may charge the visit fee **[[check: missed visit fee]]**.
