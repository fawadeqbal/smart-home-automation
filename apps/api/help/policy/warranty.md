## What is covered

**Switchboard boards** (the Wi-Fi controllers we fit behind your switchboards, with their relays and power supplies) are covered for **12 months [[check: board warranty length]]** from your installation date.

**Our installation work** (wiring we did, enclosures, wall buttons we fitted, and the setup of devices, rooms and wall buttons in the app) is covered for **6 months [[check: workmanship warranty length]]** from the installation date.

During the warranty, if a covered part fails in normal use, we repair or replace it **free of charge, including the visit and labour [[check: warranty visits and labour free]]**. We set the replacement up with the same devices, rooms and wall buttons. You may need to recreate timers and scenes that used the old board.

You don't need a paper receipt. Your home's record in our system shows its installation date and boards **[[check: no receipt needed]]**.

## What is not covered

- Damage from power surges, lightning, voltage fluctuations or a faulty UPS, inverter or generator **[[check: surge damage excluded]]**
- Water, fire, insects or rodents, and physical damage
- Boards or wiring opened, moved or changed by anyone other than our team
- Your home's existing wiring, breakers, switchboards and earthing that we didn't install
- The appliances themselves: bulbs, fans, AC, geyser, water motor and so on
- Wi-Fi and internet problems: your router, internet provider or a weak signal. We'll advise on an extender **[[check: extender supplied at extra cost]]**.
- Sensors used outside their intended conditions, for example outdoors without protection

## Software and updates

Firmware updates for your boards and updates to the apps are included for as long as your home uses Switchboard **[[check: updates free for life of service]]**. They are installed over Wi-Fi and don't need a visit.

## How to claim

1. Tell us from the app: **Me** → **Message your expert**, or **Diagnostics** → **Send to support**.
2. We check the board's health remotely first. Many problems are fixed without a visit.
3. If a part needs replacing, we book a visit (see "Service visits and charges").

## After the warranty

Repairs and replacements are charged as described in "Service visits and charges".
