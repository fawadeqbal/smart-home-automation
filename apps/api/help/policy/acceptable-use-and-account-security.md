## One account per person

Each person in a home gets their own account through an invite link. **Don't share your password or your account**. Invite the person instead, so that their access can be limited to the right rooms and removed later.

## Keeping your account safe

- Use a password you don't use elsewhere. Switchboard requires at least 8 characters with a number or symbol, and refuses common passwords.
- After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes. If this happens and it wasn't you, tell us.
- **Sign out** on shared or borrowed phones and computers.
- **Our staff will never ask for your password [[check: staff never ask for passwords]]**. We don't need it to help you.
- If you think someone else has your password, tell us straight away **[[check: response to suspected account compromise]]**.

## The owner's responsibilities

The home owner decides who can use the home and what they may do:
- Give members and guests only the rooms they need.
- **Guest access doesn't end by itself.** Remove guests, tenants, staff or former family members from **People** when they leave.
- Keep the owner's own email and phone up to date with us **[[check: how owners update contact details]]**.

## Not allowed

- Trying to open homes, accounts or boards that aren't yours, or to get around room access.
- Tampering with, re-flashing or moving the boards, or connecting your own devices to our server.
- Using Switchboard to watch or control someone without their knowledge **[[check: wording on misuse against household members]]**.
- Overloading the service, for example with automated scripts, or copying the software.
- Using the assistant or app to switch appliances in a way that endangers people (see "Electrical safety").

## What we may do

If an account is misused, or a home's access looks compromised, we may **disable the account or pause access [[check: suspension rights]]** and contact the owner. A disabled account is signed out everywhere.

## Staff

Staff access to customer homes is limited by role. Installers see only their assigned homes, and support can only look, not change anything. Every change staff make is recorded in the home's **Activity**. Staff must not use customer access for anything other than serving that customer **[[check: staff confidentiality rule]]**.
