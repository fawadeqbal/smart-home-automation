Mains electricity (220 V) can kill. These rules protect you and our team.

## How we work

- We switch off the breaker before touching any wiring, and test that the circuit is dead.
- Every board and relay is mounted inside a **closed enclosure** or switchboard. No exposed wiring is left behind.
- New boards are tested on a low-voltage lamp or LED before mains is connected.
- Only trained team members **[[check: electrician qualification required]]** wire mains connections.

## What we won't do

- Work on the **main incoming supply, meter or service cable**. That belongs to your electricity company (LESCO, K-Electric, IESCO and others) **[[check: list of DISCOs]]**.
- Connect a load bigger than the relay is rated for. **Heavy loads such as AC, geyser and water motor** are only switched through a properly rated contactor or relay **[[check: heavy-load rule and contactor charge]]**.
- Install on wiring that is unsafe: burnt, loose, or without a working breaker **[[check: earthing required]]**. We tell you what needs fixing first, and can quote for it.
- Fit boards in bathrooms, outdoors or other wet places without a suitable waterproof enclosure **[[check: wet-area rule]]**.
- Leave a job half-wired or a switchboard open.

## Sensors are not safety alarms

Leak, gas, smoke, door and motion sensors on Switchboard are **convenience alerts**. They depend on power, Wi-Fi and the internet, and they are **not a replacement for certified smoke or gas alarms** **[[check: wording of sensor disclaimer]]**. Night alerts for doors and motion are sent between 10 pm and 6 am only.

## What we ask of you

- **Don't open switchboards** or touch the boards, relays or their wiring. Ask us.
- Don't let anyone else (another electrician, a handyman) change our wiring. It ends the warranty on that work.
- If you see sparks or smoke, smell burning, hear buzzing, or a switchboard feels hot: **switch off the breaker for that circuit**, stop using it, and call us or an electrician.
- Don't block the airflow around enclosures.

## Remote control and timers

Switching from the app or a timer switches the appliance **without anyone being next to it**. Don't put appliances that are unsafe left unattended on timers or remote control, such as an iron or a heater near curtains **[[check: list of appliances we won't automate]]**.
