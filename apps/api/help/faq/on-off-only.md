**Not today.** Switchboard switches things **on and off**. Each device is connected through a relay on a board, and a relay is either on or off.

So:
- **Lights, fans, sockets, a water motor, a geyser** and similar can be turned on and off, from the app, by voice, at the wall and on timers. Your installer decides which appliances are suitable and how they're connected.
- **Fan speed and dimming aren't available.** A fan's own regulator keeps controlling its speed.
- **AC temperature and mode** (for example "24 degrees, cool") can't be set. Switchboard doesn't send remote-control signals.
- **No colours or scenes inside a bulb.**

If you ask the assistant "AC 24 pe kar do" or "pankha tez karo", it can't do it. It can switch the device on or off if it's connected.

**What Switchboard can read** (if your installer fitted the sensors):
- **Temperature and humidity**, per room
- **Doors and windows**: open or closed
- **Motion**
- **Water leaks**
- **Levels** such as light, gas, soil moisture or water level, shown as a percentage

You can use these with alerts (for example a leak, or a door opened at night) and in the app. Switchboard can't yet switch something automatically **because** a sensor changed, for example "turn on the light when the door opens". Automations are timers, daily schedules and scenes.

Questions about what's possible in your home? Ask at the free visit, or message your installer.
