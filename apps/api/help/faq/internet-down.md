**The wall buttons keep working.** Each board stores its devices and buttons in its own memory and handles button presses itself, with no Wi-Fi or internet needed.

What stops until the connection is back:
- **Switching from the app or by voice.** The apps and the assistant reach your boards through the Switchboard server on the internet, even when your phone is on the same home Wi-Fi.
- **Timers and schedules.** They run on the server. A timer that is due while a board is offline fails, and you're notified. It isn't repeated later.
- **Live states and alerts** for that home.

When the internet comes back:
- The boards **reconnect by themselves**, usually within a minute or two. You don't need to do anything.
- Each board reports where its devices ended up, so the app shows the right state even if you used the wall buttons meanwhile.
- Your devices, rooms, timers and scenes are all still there.

If only **your phone** has no internet but your home does, the boards stay online. Switch from another phone, or wait until your phone is connected again.
