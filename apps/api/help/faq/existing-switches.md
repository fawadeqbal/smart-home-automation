Switchboard is fitted **behind your existing switchboards**. Your installer places a small Wi-Fi board there and wires your lights, fans and sockets through it. The switchboards stay where they are.

**Wall control:** Switchboard's wall control uses **push-buttons**. You press once to turn something on, and press again to turn it off. Each button is wired to the board, which toggles the device itself, so it works even without Wi-Fi or internet. After a press, the app shows "wall switch" next to that device.

Whether your current switches can be used as they are, or some need to be changed to push-buttons, depends on your switchboards. Your installer tells you at the free visit, and it's included in the price you're quoted.

Good to know:
- Not every device has to have a wall button. Some are controlled from the app and by voice only. Ask your installer to add buttons where you want them.
- Each wall button controls one device on the same board.
- The app, voice and wall button all work on the same device, and the app always shows its current state.
