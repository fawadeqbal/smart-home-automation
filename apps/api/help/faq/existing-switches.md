Switchboard is fitted **behind your existing switchboards**. Your installer places a small Wi-Fi board there and wires your lights, fans and sockets through it. The switchboards stay where they are.

**Your ordinary wall switches keep working.** Your installer wires each one to the board as an input. Every flip, up or down, toggles its light, like a two-way staircase switch. So it always works, even after you switched the light from the app, and even without Wi-Fi or internet. After a flip, the app shows "wall switch" next to that device.

**Push-buttons** (the bell type that springs back) work too: one press toggles the light. A push-button can also do more through the server:
- a **double-press** can run a scene, for example "Good night",
- a **long press** can switch everything in a room, or the whole home, off,
- a button by the bed or the door can control lights on another board.

Good to know:
- Not every device has to have a wall switch. Some are controlled from the app and by voice only.
- A wall switch or button toggles one device on its own board by itself. Anything more (scenes, other boards, a room off) needs Wi-Fi.
- The app, voice and wall switch all work on the same device, and the app always shows its current state.
