title: How much internet data does Switchboard use?

Very little. The boards send short text messages, never pictures, video or sound.

**What a board sends over your home Wi-Fi:**
- a short **health report once a minute** (signal strength, voltage, memory);
- a tiny **"still here" signal** about every 15 seconds when nothing else is being sent, so the server knows it's online;
- a message **whenever something is switched**, from the app, by voice, at the wall or by a timer;
- **sensor readings**: by default, one temperature reading every 30 seconds per temperature sensor, and one message whenever a door, motion or leak sensor changes;
- the occasional **firmware update**, under 1 MB, only when you or your installer install one.

All together this is small compared with normal phone use. Watching a few minutes of video uses more data than a board does in a long time.

**The apps** use a little data to load and to show live updates. **Voice** sends each short recording (a few seconds) to the server and gets a short spoken reply back. Hands-free listening doesn't use data while it waits for "Hey Switchboard". The wake word is recognised on your device.

If your home internet has a monthly data limit, Switchboard won't noticeably affect it. You do need an internet connection that is **always on**, so you can control your home from the app and your timers can run.
