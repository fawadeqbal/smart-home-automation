Only people you invite can open your home, and only with the rights you give them.

**Your account**
- Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash, so nobody, including us, can read them.
- After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes, which stops guessing.
- Sign-in uses secure cookies. A session ends after 30 days without use, or when you sign out.
- Passwords need at least 8 characters with a number or symbol, and common passwords are refused.

**Who can do what**
- Each person's access is set room by room: **Can see** or **Can switch**. The assistant follows the same rights, so it can never do more than the person speaking.
- Members and guests can't see rooms that aren't shared with them.
- Our staff are limited too: your installer works only on homes assigned to them, and support staff can look but not change anything.
- Every change (invites, access, devices, staff work) is recorded in your home's **Activity**.

**Your boards**
- Boards connect to our server over an **encrypted connection**, and check that they're talking to the real server.
- Each board may only send and receive its own messages.
- A board's Wi-Fi password is sent only to the board. It isn't stored or shown in the app.

**Voice**
- The "Hey Switchboard" wake word is recognised on your own device. Nothing is sent before it.
- Recordings aren't saved. See "Privacy and your data".

**What you can do**
- Don't share your password. Invite people instead.
- Remove guests and anyone who has left from **People**. Guest access doesn't end by itself.
- Sign out on shared devices.
- If something looks wrong in **Activity**, tell your installer straight away.
