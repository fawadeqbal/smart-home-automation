**Yes, the assistant does.** You can speak or type in:
- **English**: "turn off the bedroom fan"
- **Roman Urdu**: "bedroom ka pankha band karo", "batti jala do", "pankha 10 minute baad band kar do"
- **Urdu script**
- **A mix of both**: "kitchen ki light off kar do"

Common words are understood: batti, pankha, band karo, chala do, jala do, bujha do, minute baad, roz, subah, raat, baje. Your installer can add the names your family uses for a device, such as "tube light" or "motor", as **other names for voice**.

How it answers:
- It replies in the language you used. Roman Urdu gets Roman Urdu, and Urdu script gets Urdu script.
- Short everyday commands are confirmed instantly in English, for example "Done, the bulb is off."
- Spoken replies use an English voice. Replies in Urdu script are read in a simpler, more robotic voice.
- Speech recognition listens for English and Urdu only. If it mistakes Urdu for Hindi, it listens again as Urdu.

**The app screens are in English.** Buttons, menus and messages aren't translated yet.

If voice misunderstands you, try typing the same request, or say the device's name exactly as it appears on its tile. See "The assistant can't hear me, misunderstands me or uses the wrong language".
