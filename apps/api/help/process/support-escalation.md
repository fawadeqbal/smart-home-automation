## How problems reach us

1. **Automatically.** When a board in a home goes offline or comes back, or reports low voltage or very weak Wi-Fi, the home's owners and admins **and its installer** get a notification.
2. **From the customer.** Owners and admins use **Me → Message your expert** (phone app) or **Diagnostics → Send to support** (web). This creates a **Help requested · <home>** notification for the home's **installer**, every **support** user and every **super admin**. It links to the home's Diagnostics (and the board, if they picked one). The message is also saved in the home's **Activity**.
3. **From the family.** Members and guests can't send requests. They go through the owner.

The app has no reply channel. Answer by phone or WhatsApp.

## Who does what

| Step | Who | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. First look | **Support** or the **installer** | Open the notification → home workspace → **Diagnostics**, the board's **Overview**, health charts and **Live log**. Support is read-only on homes and boards: it can look at everything but can't change wiring, Wi-Fi or firmware. |
| 2. Fix | **Installer** of that home | Restart the board, move it to new Wi-Fi (**Wi-Fi** tab), fix devices and pins, install firmware, manage rooms, or send the owner invite. Plan a site visit if needed. |
| 3. Escalate | **Super admin** | Everything the installer can do, plus: assign a replacement board, **Mark faulty**, upload firmware builds, change the home's installer, invite or remove people in the home, and handle team and inventory. |

## When to escalate to a super admin

- A board has to be replaced, or a new one added.
- The home needs a different installer, or the installer is unavailable.
- Account problems the owner can't fix themselves, such as a lost owner password or a change of owner. The app has no button for these.
- Anything that looks like a server problem: "The voice assistant is not running", every home offline at once, or the site down.
- Policy questions (warranty, charges, refunds).

## Good habits

- Check **Activity** first. It shows recent changes by the family and by staff.
- Before marking a board faulty, note its devices and pins (see "Mark a board faulty and replace it").
- Keep your notes on the call in the visit request or your team's usual channel. Support notifications are kept for 30 days.
