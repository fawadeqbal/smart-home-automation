## 1. Book a free visit

On the Switchboard website (https://home.fawadiqbal.dev), go to **Book a free visit**. Fill in your **Name** and **Phone**, choose your **City** (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad or Somewhere else) and your **Home size** (Apartment, 5 Marla, 10 Marla or 1 Kanal+), then press **Book my free visit**.

The confirmation says our team will call you within one working day to pick a time. If you see "Too many requests from here — please try again in an hour, or call us.", the form has been used too often from your connection. Wait an hour or call us.

## 2. The visit

Our expert walks through your home with you, asks what you'd like to control, and gives you a clear price before any work starts. The visit is free and there's no obligation.

## 3. Installation day

Your installer:
- fits a small Wi-Fi board behind each switchboard you chose, connects it to your home Wi-Fi, and wires your lights, fans, sockets and any sensors to it;
- names every device and puts it in the right room;
- adds wall buttons where you want them, and tests everything from the app and the wall.

You'll need your **Wi-Fi name and password** on the day. Boards use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.

## 4. Handover

Your installer sends **you, the home owner**, an invite link (usually on WhatsApp). Open it, choose a password, and your home is ready. A short tour shows you around. Then invite your family from **People** (web) or **Me** → **Family** (phone app), and choose which rooms each person can use.

## After that

Your installer stays your contact. They're told automatically when a board goes offline. You can message them from the app at any time: **Me** → **Message your expert**, or **Diagnostics** → **Send to support**.

See also the policies on warranty, service visits and support hours.
