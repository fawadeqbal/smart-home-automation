The last setup step is **Handed over to owner**. The owner gets everything in the home (switching, scenes, voice, rooms, inviting the family), while boards, pins and wiring stay with you.

## Before you send it

- Every device is in a room and has been tested from the app and the wall.
- The rooms have names the family recognises.
- You've used **See what the owner sees** to check the home looks right.

## Send the owner invite

1. In the home's workspace, press **Send owner invite** on **Overview → Setup** (step 6). Installers also find **Send owner invite** on the **People** tab.
2. Enter the **owner's email**, which becomes their sign-in, and their name. Press **Create invite link**.
3. Copy the link and send it to the owner on WhatsApp. Switchboard doesn't send emails or texts.
4. The link is valid **7 days** and works once. If the owner already has a Switchboard account with that email, they confirm their existing password.

If the home was created with an owner email, the link already exists. Use **Resend invite** to make a fresh one. A new owner invite always replaces the unused old one.

## Rules

- **Installers can only send the owner invite.** The owner invites everyone else. Super admins can invite anyone.
- The owner must be the customer. An email that belongs to someone on your team is refused: "The owner must be the customer, not someone on your team."
- A home has exactly one owner. Once the owner has joined, no new owner invite can be sent.

## After they join

- Step 6 turns done, and the home's status becomes **Live** if its boards are healthy.
- On first sign-in the owner gets a short tour. It covers their rooms, trying a light, inviting family, choosing alerts and adding the app to the home screen.
- Show the owner how to message you: **Me** → **Message your expert** (phone app) or **Diagnostics** → **Send to support** (web).
- Until the owner joins, the console shows **Owner hasn't joined** under *Needs attention*.
