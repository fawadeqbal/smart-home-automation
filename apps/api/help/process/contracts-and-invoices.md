Open **Contracts & invoices** in the console. Super admins make and change contracts and bills. Support can see them.

## A maintenance contract

1. Press **New contract** and pick the home.
2. Choose a starting point (**Basic care**, **Care plan**, **Annual care**). These are examples: change the name and price to yours.
3. Set **Every** (month, quarter or year), the **visits included** each period, and the **start** date. An end date is optional.
4. Press **Start contract**.

The first bill is made on the start date. After that, one is made every period by itself. Each bill is due **10 days** after it's made. The home's owner and admins get a notification for each new bill, and an email if email is set up. A home can have one contract at a time.

- **Pause** stops new bills (for example, while the family is abroad). **Resume** starts again from today. It doesn't bill the paused months.
- **End** stops the contract for good. To change the plan later, end it and make a new one.

## One-off bills

Press **One-off bill** for a service visit, new work or parts. Write what it's for, the amount, and when it's due.

## Recording a payment

Customers pay the way you agree with them: cash to the installer, bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. The app doesn't take payments itself. When the money arrives:

1. Under **To collect**, press **Paid** next to the bill.
2. Choose **How** it was paid, add the transaction ID or receipt number if there is one, and the date.
3. Press **Record payment**. The home is told it was received.

Marked paid by mistake? Press the ↺ button next to it. **×** cancels a bill that shouldn't have been made.

## Reminders

A bill that's still unpaid a day after its due date is marked **Overdue**. The home's owner and admins get a reminder then, and again every week until it's paid or cancelled.

## Sending a bill

- **Send** opens WhatsApp with the bill's number, what it's for, the amount and the due date.
- Click the bill's number to open it as a page. **Print or save as PDF** makes a printable invoice with your company name, logo (added in the company tour) and Tax ID (set it in **Company**, or **Plan & billing**).

The home sees its plan and bills under **Plan & bills**.
