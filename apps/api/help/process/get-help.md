Your installer is your home expert. They set up your home and look after it, and they're told automatically when one of your boards goes offline.

## Ask for help in the app

Every question or problem becomes a **help request** with a number (#12). Your installer's team answers in the same request, and you get a notification for each answer.

**Web app**
1. Open **Help requests** in the menu and press **Ask for help**. Owners and admins can also press **Send to support** in **Diagnostics**, which adds a link to the board.
2. Pick what it's about, write what's wrong, and press **Send**.
3. The answers appear in the request. Reply there. When it's fixed, press **It's solved**. If the problem comes back, press **Open it again**.

**Phone app**
- Owners and admins: **Me** → **Message your expert**, then **Send**.
- Everyone: **Me** → **Help requests** shows your requests and their answers. You can reply there.

**By voice:** "tell the installer the bedroom fan isn't working". The assistant reads back what it will send and sends it when you say yes.

A request that says **Waiting for you** needs your answer before the team can go on.

## Who sees what

- Members and guests see their own requests. Owners and admins see every request from the home.
- Your installer's team sees your requests, with your home's boards and history, so they can often fix things remotely.
- For water, sparks or smoke, the request is marked urgent automatically. Switch that board off at the DB first.

## What to include

- What isn't working: which room and which device.
- Since when, and whether the wall button still works.
- Any message the app showed, word for word.

## Before you send

Many problems fix themselves or have a quick fix:
- A room shows **Offline**: see "A board is offline". Restarting the router often helps.
- New router or Wi-Fi password: see "I changed my Wi-Fi or got a new router".
- A device **did not respond**: see "A device doesn't respond".

## Emergencies

If you see sparks or smoke, smell burning, or a switchboard feels hot, **switch off the breaker** for that circuit first. Then call your installer or an electrician. Don't wait for a reply in the app.

Support hours and response times are in the support policy.
