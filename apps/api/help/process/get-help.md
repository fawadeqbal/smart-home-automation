Your installer is your home expert. They set up your home and look after it, and they're told automatically when one of your boards goes offline.

## Send a message from the app (owners and admins)

**Phone app**
1. Go to **Me** → **Message your expert**. It shows your installer's first name.
2. Under **What's wrong? A sentence is enough.**, describe it. For example: "The kitchen lights don't switch from the app since this morning."
3. Press **Send**.

**Web app**
1. Open **Diagnostics** and press **Send to support**.
2. Under **Which board?**, choose the board the problem is about, or **The whole home**.
3. Write **What's wrong?** and press **Send**.

Your installer and the Switchboard support team get a notice with a link straight to your home's diagnostics. They'll contact you by phone or WhatsApp. The app doesn't show replies.

## Members and guests

Members and guests can't send support messages. Tell the home owner, who can send one, or change your access if that's the problem ("Need another room? Ask …" in the phone app).

## What to include

- What isn't working: which room and which device.
- Since when, and whether the wall button still works.
- Any message the app showed, word for word.

## Before you send

Many problems fix themselves or have a quick fix:
- A room shows **Offline**: see "A board is offline". Restarting the router often helps.
- New router or Wi-Fi password: see "I changed my Wi-Fi or got a new router".
- A device **did not respond**: see "A device doesn't respond".

## Emergencies

If you see sparks or smoke, smell burning, or a switchboard feels hot, **switch off the breaker** for that circuit first. Then call your installer or an electrician. Don't wait for a reply in the app.

Support hours and response times are in the support policy.
