Super admins create homes. Installers then see the home in **My jobs** once they are its installer.

## Create the home

1. Go to **Console → Homes** and press **New home**. Or, from a visit request, use **Create their home** (the name and city are filled in).
2. Fill in the **New home** form:
   - **Home name**, for example "Khan residence". The owner sees this name.
   - **City**.
   - **Time zone**: keep **Asia/Karachi** for Pakistan. Timers, schedules and the night alerts (10 pm – 6 am) use it.
   - **Installer**: pick who does the job, or "Not yet".
   - **Rooms**: comma separated. The default is "Living room, Kitchen, Bedroom". You can change them later.
   - **Owner's name** (optional) and **Owner's email**. With an email, an owner invite link is created straight away.
3. Press **Create home**.
4. The result shows the **Owner invite link (valid 7 days)**. Copy it, or keep it for handover day. Then press **Open workspace** or **Assign boards**.

## The home's workspace

Every home has a workspace with the tabs **Overview**, **Boards**, **Rooms**, **People**, **Diagnostics** and **Activity**. The **Setup** list on Overview tracks six steps and marks them done as the work happens, in any order:
1. **Home created**
2. **Boards assigned**: picked from inventory (super admin)
3. **Boards installed and online**: the installer powers them on site
4. **Devices and pins configured**: lights, sensors, wall buttons
5. **Rooms and access**
6. **Handed over to owner**: the owner accepts the invite

**See what the owner sees** opens the home exactly as the customer sees it.

## Editing later

**Edit** (super admin) changes the name, city, time zone and installer. **Archived** hides the home from lists; people keep their accounts.

## Rules to know

- The owner must be the customer. The app refuses an owner email that belongs to someone on your team.
- A home has one owner. A new owner invite replaces any unused one.
- One installer per home. Assigning a board with a different installer moves the whole home to that installer.
