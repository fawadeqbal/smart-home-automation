Bookings from the website's **Book a free visit** form appear in **Console → Visit requests**. A live badge in the menu shows how many are waiting, and they also appear under *Needs attention* on **Overview**. Super admins and support can see and update them.

## Statuses

| Status | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **New** | Waiting for a call |
| **Called** | You've spoken to them, but no visit yet |
| **Visit booked** | A date is set |
| **Customer** | Their home is in the console (set automatically when you create it) |
| **Not now** | Not interested, or no answer |

## Step by step

1. Open **Visit requests**. The **To follow up** filter shows New, Called and Visit booked. You can search by name, phone or city.
2. Pick a request. The panel shows the city, the home size and when they asked.
3. **Call** them, or press **WhatsApp**. Local numbers starting with 03… are converted to +92 for WhatsApp automatically.
4. Set **Where it stands**, for example **Called**, then **Visit booked**.
5. Write what was agreed under **Notes** (only your team sees these), for example "Visit Saturday 4 pm · wants motor on a timer", and press **Save note**.
6. After the visit, if they go ahead, a super admin presses **Create their home**. The new home form opens with the name and city filled in (see "Create a new customer home"). Saving marks the request as **Customer** and links the home.

The website promises the customer a call **within one working day**. Work through **New** requests first.

## Good to know

- Each change records who handled it ("Handled by").
- The form takes at most 5 requests an hour from one visitor, and silently drops bots.
- Nothing is deleted. Use **Not now** to close a request.
- Support staff can update statuses and notes, but only a super admin can create the home.
